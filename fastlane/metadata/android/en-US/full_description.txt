Fenfa Watch Manager is a standalone Android companion app that connects directly to your Wear OS smartwatch via Wireless ADB.

It enables you to manage, install, and update Wear OS applications hosted on your self-hosted Fenfa server — completely without a computer, cables, or third-party ADB tools.

Features:
• Direct Wireless ADB connection to Wear OS watches (tested on Galaxy Watch 8 with Wear OS 6 / One UI 8 Watch).
• Automatic mDNS discovery of watches with active wireless debugging on your local Wi-Fi.
• Multi-app management with live version comparison against your Fenfa instance.
• Dual-host connectivity (LAN Wi-Fi and Remote VPN / Tailscale).
• 1-click silent APK installation and updates directly onto your watch via ADB shell.
• Persistent RSA keypair stored securely in internal app storage.
• 100% Free and Open Source (GPLv3), with no telemetry, tracking, or ads.
